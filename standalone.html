02The Mediterranean
A little further, a little slower.

There is another way to get there.

Past the last town, the road folds into the hillside. The sea takes over. For once, there is nothing to do but follow the curve.

04

An unhurried kind of day

The distance
between two places.

We had no destination. Just a road beside the water, a folded map on the dashboard, and an entire afternoon ahead of us.

Shutters were half closed. A chair stood in a square of shade. Lunch was still happening behind an open window.

We stopped for a view. For a coffee. For no reason we could name. With every pause, the day grew a little wider.

Perhaps taking the long way gives the things in between a chance to become the things you remember.

06

Things to take with you

Field notes
for slow days.

1.

Leave the morning open.

One good coffee. A walk without a route. Let the day tell you where to go.

2.

Stop when you notice something.

A patch of light, a quiet cove, the smell of bread. Small things make a place.

3.

Bring back a feeling.

You do not have to photograph everything. Some moments can stay yours.
